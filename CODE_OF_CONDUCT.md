# Code of Conduct

This project is a free resource for photographers, editors and anyone learning how photo metadata works. Everyone who takes part, by opening issues, suggesting changes or sharing their own copies, is expected to keep it welcoming.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and assume good intent. People come here at every skill level.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Credit others' photos, captions and code when you share or adapt them.
- Keep client information private. Never post real client contacts, bookings or unpublished photos in issues or examples.

## Not acceptable
- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, or using the project to promote unrelated products.

## Reporting
Report a problem through the contact form at [ashergreenephoto.com](https://ashergreenephoto.com). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, close issues or block anyone who doesn't follow this code.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
